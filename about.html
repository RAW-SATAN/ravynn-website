About Us

The Ravynn Philosophy

Who We Are

Ravynn was created for individuals who move differently — who don't follow noise, trends, or crowds. Built on minimal aesthetics and oversized comfort, every piece is designed to feel effortless yet unforgettable.

Why We Started

Ravynn began with a simple frustration: most "oversized" streetwear in India was either overpriced imports or thin, see-through basics that lost their shape after two washes. We wanted tees that actually held up — heavyweight, well-cut, and built for everyday wear, at a price that made sense.

What started as a small batch of designs for friends has grown into a full catalog, but the standard hasn't changed.

Why 240 GSM Matters

GSM (grams per square metre) measures fabric weight — and it's the biggest difference between a t-shirt that drapes well and one that feels flimsy. Most fast-fashion tees sit around 150–180 GSM.

Ravynn tees are cut from 240 GSM cotton, which means a heavier drop, a fuller oversized silhouette, and fabric that resists going sheer or misshapen after repeated washes. Combined with bio-washing for softness and DTF (direct-to-film) printing for graphics that don't crack or fade, it's a fabric built to actually last in your rotation.

How They're Made

Every Ravynn tee is bio-washed for a soft, broken-in feel from the first wear, cut for an oversized fit that still holds its shape, and finished with fade-resistant DTF prints. We work with small-batch manufacturing partners in India, which keeps quality control tight and lets us move fast on new drops instead of sitting on mass-produced inventory.

Move Differently

Ravynn isn't about logos for the sake of logos. It's for people who'd rather wear something that says what they actually think — original graphics, honest typography, and the occasional Hindi line that hits harder in your mother tongue than in English.

No noise, no crowd-following. Just pieces built for how you actually move.
